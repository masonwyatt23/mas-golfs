# Contributing to Mas Golfs

## What this is

An open-source autonomous golf world. A +3 digital-twin golfer plays 18-hole rounds on repeat, forever, built to be watched and livestreamed.

## Where to start

- **Shot model** — launch-condition physics, dispersion calibration, mishit behavior, lie/wind gating. The truth tables live in `docs/architecture.md`.
- **Director logic** — camera cuts, drama scoring, replay triggers.
- **Broadcast graphics** — scorebug, leaderboard, lower-thirds, ticker.
- **The world** — course design, terrain, vegetation, sky, lighting.

## Ground rules

- The golfer is a +3. Calibrate to real scratch/+3 benchmarks, not Tour numbers. If he drives it 300 and hits 75% of greens, it's wrong.
- The show must play itself. No human input required at any point — open it and it plays.
- Clubhouse Coins stay closed-loop play money: free, earned by watching, never purchasable, never convertible to anything of value. No real-money or crypto features.
- Keep the Mason W Golf / Mas Golfs identity intact. The golfer is Mas, Mason's digital twin.
